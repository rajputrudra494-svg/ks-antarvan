# K's Antarvan — research notes

Gathered 2026-10-13. Everything below is split into **verified** (two or more
independent public sources agree) and **unverified** (single source, or my own
reading of the photos in `assets/img/`). Copy on the site should only state the
verified things as fact.

## The name

**अंतर्वन / antarvan** — *antar* (within) + *van* (forest): "the forest within."
The chefs' jackets in `chef-1.jpg` … `chef-5.jpg` carry the wordmark
**K' अंतर्वन** in Devanagari, so the Devanagari lockup is part of the brand and
is safe to use on the site.

12 PM – 8 PM; Restaurant Guru shows 12–3 PM and 7 PM–12 AM; Snapchat and the

tells people to call ahead. Worth confirming with the restaurant before launch.

## The spaces (read from the photos — unverified wording)

Two distinct rooms, which is what the image set is organised around:

- **The glasshouse** (`interior-glasshouse`, `glass-roof`, `chandelier`,
  `lamp-wall`, `arches-rain`, `rain-window`, `hero-rain`, `lamp-rain`) — a
  conservatory: pitched glass roof on green steel, gothic arched glazing, sheer
  cream drapes, scalloped table and floor lamps, green-and-white chequerboard
  marble floor, planting pressed against the glass on every side. Reads
  beautifully in the rain, which is what most of the hero photography shows.
- **The outhouse** (`outhouse-garden`, `outhouse-pause`, `outhouse-sketch`) — the
  garden: a curved slatted-wood bench wrapped around a tree, green mosaic-tiled
  tables, gravel underfoot, festoon lights, dense bamboo and gulmohar.

`glasshouse-illustration.png` and `outhouse-sketch.png` are dark-green engraving
style illustrations with transparent backgrounds — used on the site as section
ornaments over cream, not as photos.

The chef photographs (`chef-1` … `chef-5`) are black and white, shot in the pass
and on the sushi station. Nothing public names the chef, so the site does not
name one.

## Menus

Both PDFs are in `assets/menus/` and linked from the menu page. Plain-text
extractions are in `_research/menu-text/` — these are the source of truth for
`js/menu-data.js`.

- Food: soups, salads, house specials, hot stone bowls (new), appetizers
  (Lebanese / Indian / Mexican / Asian), noodles, sushi, dim sum & bao, mains
  (Asian / Indian), rice, breads, kid's special, desserts.
- Beverages: hot coffee, iced coffee, matcha, classics, teas, shakes, mocktails,
  aerated drinks.
- Markers printed on the menu: spicy, new in the menu, available in Jain,
  available in no onion & garlic. Carried through to the site.
- **No prices are printed in either PDF**, so the site shows none.
- Vegan milk available at +₹100. "If you have any food allergies, please inform
  our team" appears on every page — kept on the site.
