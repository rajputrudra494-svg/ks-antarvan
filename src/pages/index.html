A room built to let the garden in

The name says what the room does. Green steel ribs, arched glazing, sheer drapes and a chequerboard marble floor — and beyond every pane, planting pressed right up against the glass.

Lamps come on early here, so late lunch and dinner feel like the same soft hour. The kitchen is pure vegetarian from end to end, and it cooks in several languages at once: hot stone bowls and dim sum, a sushi counter, a tandoor, hummus and empanadas, and a dessert list that ends in payasam.
